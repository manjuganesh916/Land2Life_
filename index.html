How it works

A calm guide for a complicated process

01Set your situation

State, land status and income help the scheme finder rank useful options.

02Check documents

The pre-check catches obvious mistakes. Nothing you type is uploaded.

03Plan the money

Balance grant, savings and loan. Look closely at the funding gap.

04Track every stage

Keep acknowledgment receipts and note the office and official you meet.

Watch out for middlemen

Ask for the official form, a written list of documents and a receipt for any payment. Unofficial “fast-track” fees are a red flag. Verify instructions at the Gram Panchayat or block office.
