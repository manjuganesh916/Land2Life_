🛡️ Top 3 Reasons Rural Housing Payments Get Blocked

  • 1. Inactive Aadhaar NPCI Bank Seeding: Even with an open bank account, PFMS Fund Transfer Orders (FTOs) fail if Aadhaar is not mapped to the NPCI mapper at your bank branch or India Post Payments Bank.
  • 2. Geo-Duplicate Site Conflict: Under RGRHCL / Basava Vasati rules, if an old foundation or previous beneficiary photo was captured at the same GPS coordinates, the system flags a "Geo Duplicate".
  • 3. Building Above Plinth Before 1st Geo-Tag: Always ask the Gram Rozgar Sahayak / PDO to capture the AwaasApp photo at each exact stage (Vacant Site → Plinth → Lintel → Roof).
